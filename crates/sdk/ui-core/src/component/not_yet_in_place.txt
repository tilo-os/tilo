# Described widgets that a change to their own props still builds again, because they take no
# props yet (`set_props` is the trait's default, which refuses) or because the parts that change
# in them are what they are built around. A rebuild costs the person what they were doing with
# the widget — the focus in a field, a drag under a finger, the menu a press opened — so each of
# these owes a `set_props` that takes its changing parts where it stands.
#
# The list only shrinks: a widget that takes its props in place must leave it, and no widget may
# join it.
