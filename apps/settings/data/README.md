Physical outlines and coordinates derived from xkeyboard-config geometry/pc models pc104, pc105, jp106 and kr106, The Open Group 1996/1998. Source SHA-256: 76a8d3c15451770719f5ad6b817ac18cb1496ecde9b165763af2ba57cda55336. Coordinates are millimetres; ISO Enter preserves the original six-point outline. Symbols are supplied separately by the current native XKB compiler, never by this geometry. These explicit preview models do not claim hardware detection. License: pc-keyboard-geometry.LICENSE.

The additional models use the first (outer) shape outline, section/row offsets and per-key gaps from the same pinned source. Their exact key counts are 106 each. JIS includes AE13, AB11, MUHE, HENK and HKTG; Korean includes HNGL and HJCV. The derivation was checked against the existing pc104/pc105 coordinates before adding the two models.

`world-land.b64`: the Date & Time map's land masses, from Natural Earth's 1:110m physical "land" polygons (`ne_110m_land.geojson`, natural-earth-vector, source SHA-256 9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9). Each polygon's outer ring, with consecutive duplicate points dropped and rings wholly south of 58°S (Antarctica, below the map) left out, is stored, base64-encoded, as a little-endian u16 point count, the first point as two i16 (longitude, latitude) in tenths of a degree, then one i8 pair per further point holding the step from the point before. Natural Earth is in the public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
