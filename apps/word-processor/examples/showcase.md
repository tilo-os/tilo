# Room to think

Good ideas need room to breathe. Bring **clear thinking** and shared context to every draft.

## Write together

Keep decisions close to the words. Shape the argument together, then leave a clear next step.

| Detail | Intention |
| --- | --- |
| A considered canvas | Room for the work |
| Comments in context | Less back and forth |

> Keep it simple. Make room for good ideas.

A thoughtful draft is an invitation: read closely, ask a better question and make something worth sharing.
